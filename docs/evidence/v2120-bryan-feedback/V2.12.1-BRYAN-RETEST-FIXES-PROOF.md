## Requirements Covered

- Event History must be visible for cross-checking completed events against pointscore.
- Breakers receive entry points only: 2 points.
- Non-breaking finishers shift up into the 5/4/3 place points when a breaker is ahead of them.
- Everyone who enters but does not place receives 2 points, including brace/relay teams.
- Manual placing no longer needs a separate "Tap Placing" start/done mode.

## Implementation

- `src/pointscore.js`
  - Added breaker-aware individual heat scoring.
  - Breakers get `2` entry points and do not consume 5/4/3 scoring places.
  - Non-breakers are compressed upward for scoring points.
  - Relay/brace/team `finisherPoints` changed from `0` to `2`.
- `src/public/js/screens/results.js`
  - Removed the separate Tap Placing mode button and done banner.
  - Manual cells are direct tap targets: tap empty cell to assign next manual place, tap assigned cell to clear.
  - Finish-time cells remain available for time entry.
- `src/public/js/screens/pointscore.js`
  - Added Event History to More reports.
  - Added Event History buttons to the three main reports for direct cross-checking.
- Version/cache-bust bumped from `2.12.0` to `2.12.1`.
